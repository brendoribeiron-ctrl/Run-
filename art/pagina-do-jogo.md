# Página do jogo no Roblox

Textos e imagens para colar em Creator Dashboard > sua experiência > Configure.

## Nome

```
Wall Breaker Titans
```

Alternativas, se o nome já existir: `Wall Breaker Titans [ALPHA]`, `Titan Rush: Break the Walls`.

## Descrição

```
💥 CHARGE UP. RUN THE HALLWAY. BREAK EVERY WALL! 💥

👊 Train on your punching bag to gain POWER
⚡ Hold to CHARGE, then launch down the hallway
🧱 Smash through walls: the further you go, the rarer your reward
🎰 Roll a FIGHTER after every run (Common to MYTHIC!)
💰 Place fighters on your plot and earn money every second
⭐ Upgrade Speed, Crit Chance and Crit Damage

🕹️ CONTROLS
Click / Tap: punch
E: hold to charge
Shift: sprint
Q: dash
Space x2: double jump
K: attributes

🚧 ALPHA: new content coming soon!
```
